Partner data & visual assets — beta

Approved internal testing only. These feeds are beta. Do not announce, republish, mirror, redistribute or embed them publicly yet. Public partner embeds are planned but are not production-ready.

How it works

  1. A market snapshot refreshes during the Australian market session at an approximately 20-minute cadence.
  2. It publishes self-contained JSON resources for the supplied Australian Top 200 universe, selected market content and scans.
  3. The same snapshot creates portable HTML panels and high-resolution JPEGs in light and dark variants.
  4. Use the stable latest URLs below and check the snapshot manifest for freshness before use.

For internal Excel access and live-feed viewing, Microsoft Edge is the recommended browser for the Excel web-data workflow.

Rendered HTML panels

Base URL: https://jmrktch.github.io/widgets/snapshot-renders/latest/

PanelURL pattern
Panel indexsnapshot-renders/latest/
Scansscans/top-gainers.html (replace the scan name as required)
Dividends and raisesdividends.html, raises.html
Heatmapheatmap.html
Benchmark chartsbenchmarks/A200.html (replace A200 with a benchmark symbol)

Panels use portable HTML, inline styling and SVG charts/sparklines. Standard tables are 1200 × 675, the heatmap is 1200 × 1200, and benchmark charts are 850 × 850. Each includes the Marketech Focus CTA and data disclaimer.

Light and dark HTML

Light is the default. Add ?dark, ?theme=dark or #dark to switch themes.

Light: top-gainers.html
Dark: top-gainers.html?theme=dark

JPEG image assets

Every panel is captured as a high-resolution JPEG in both themes. The asset manifest is images/manifest.json.

  • Light top gainers JPEG
  • Dark top gainers JPEG
  • Light heatmap JPEG
  • Dark A200 chart JPEG

Beta notes

  • Use the manifest generation time as the freshness check; latest alone is not a freshness guarantee.
  • An incomplete collection retains the prior complete snapshot rather than publishing partial data.
  • Data, output layouts, URLs and available instruments may change during beta.
  • No public syndication, public embeds or automated redistribution without written approval.
